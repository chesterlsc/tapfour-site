The idea

Happy guests would review you. Finding the page is the problem.

Leaving a Google review takes five steps on a phone, and the moment passes. The film shows those steps, strikes them out, and replaces them with one tap. Only after the stand is understood does it introduce Tapfour Connect, through the same stand: a live menu the owner changes from their phone, and a dashboard that counts every tap.

Order follows your brief: physical product → customer action → result → Connect → owner control → business value → call to action. Glossy black carries about 85% of product screen time; white appears once as the alternate finish.

Claims

What the films say, and why each line is true

LineBacked by
Your Google review page opens.The tap hits go.tap4.ph/t/<code>, which answers with a 302 (no-store) to the business’s review link.
No app.NFC and QR open a web link; guests install nothing.
Nothing to charge. No batteries, no Wi-Fi.NFC tags are passive; the redirect uses the guest’s own data connection.
Arrives programmed.Checkout collects the Google Maps link and staff build the review link before shipping.
Guests see it on their next look.The guest menu is a hosted page cached for 15 s; it doesn’t push live, so the film shows it reloading.
Every stand updates on the next tap.The product’s own confirmation text; redirects are never cached.
Reviews still come from your guests.Said on purpose. The films never claim reviews, rankings, followers, review counts, Wi-Fi or table ordering.
Before paid media

Things the audit found

SITE

“Higher ranking on Google Maps”

The hero promises a ranking outcome. The films avoid it; the site should too.

SITE

The live-taps ticker

“got a 5★ Google review”, “+12 Instagram followers”, “rating 4.4 → 4.8★” read as live events the platform can’t measure.

SITE

Wi-Fi and guest stats

Tap-to-join Wi-Fi, “in now”, “avg stay” and “returning” appear on the page and in the Solo plan but aren’t built yet.

CATALOG

Acrylic vs PVC

products.csv calls the L-Stand acrylic. The product is glossy PVC.

NAMING

“Connect” twice

The 4-in-1 face is printed “Connect with us” while the software product is Tapfour Connect.

DASHBOARD

Home overflows on phones

The owner Home lays out 510 px wide on a 390 px screen. One-line fix in platform/src/views.js: mobile .app needs grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr).

Limits

What would make it better

People. There’s no live-action footage; the phone is animated and there are no hands. A half-day pickup in a real café (a guest’s hand tapping the black stand, the owner at the counter marking an item sold out) would warm up the Reel and give the 15 s ad a human first frame.

Music. The score is original and synthesized so the films are clear to post. It was checked by measurement (spectrum, −14 LUFS), not by ear. Replace it with a licensed track if it isn’t right; the sound design stays.

Prices. None are baked in, because the site’s prices are sale-driven. Adding a price end card is one line in the edit file.
